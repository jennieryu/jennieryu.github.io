Shopcord — All-in-One Wholesale SaaS Platform

FASHIONGO's revenue was transaction-based: the platform earned when orders flowed through the marketplace. The opportunity was to become the system wholesale vendors run their entire business on — and to turn that into recurring revenue.

I built and launched Shopcord, an all-in-one SaaS platform for wholesale vendors covering inventory and line sheet management, order processing, payments and settlements, shipping, and SMS/email marketing. Monetized through subscriptions and integrated payments, it shifts FASHIONGO from transaction-based to recurring revenue. I'm now leading the Phase 2.5 expansion.

Embedded Payments · Checkout · Launched June 2026

Fashiongo Pay — Embedded Payment Solution

FASHIONGO's payment infrastructure was built to serve a single marketplace. With vendors now selling through their own Shopcord stores as well, buyers needed one way to pay everywhere — and the payment layer needed to become portable.

I modularized FASHIONGO's payment infrastructure into an embeddable checkout solution that lets buyers save and reuse payment methods across FASHIONGO and Shopcord stores. It also lays the groundwork to extend Net Terms to 2,000+ vendors as a net-new revenue vertical.

03

Logistics · Fulfillment · 0-to-1

Label It — Fulfillment Partner Portal

The LA Fashion District jobber market is substantial — but every relabeling transaction happened entirely off-platform. Vendors, buyers, and relabeling partners were coordinating by phone call, managing defects and deliveries manually, and settling fees through paper invoices.

I identified this as an untapped revenue surface and built an operations portal integrating FASHIONGO and vendor portals to bring relabeling orders onto the marketplace. The system automates order routing, defect notifications, shipping fee allocation, and multi-party settlement. We were the first wholesale platform to offer this.

Growth · Promotions

Matching Promotion Tool

In a tough economic climate, vendors wanted to run promotions to drive sales — but absorbing the full discount cost alone was a real barrier. The platform needed stronger price competitiveness to make FASHIONGO Week a genuine prime sales event.

I introduced a co-funding model where the platform matches vendor discount contributions — reducing the burden on sellers while amplifying promotional depth platform-wide. Vendor participation grew from 200 to 580, driving the highest-performing FASHIONGO Week on record. What started as a one-time event tool became a permanent marketing feature.

05

Logistics · Payments · Billing Automation

Ship with FG — End-to-End Logistics Program

Wholesale vendors were paying full market rate for every shipment — no leverage, no infrastructure, no automation. I built Ship with FG as a fully integrated logistics program: not just a shipping rate tool, but a connected system spanning carrier integrations, automated settlement, refunds, and payment processing.

By consolidating vendor shipping volume, vendors get FASHIONGO's discounted carrier rates — up to 60–70% below industry average — while the platform earns margin on the spread. Expanding across UPS, FedEx, and USPS grew program adoption from 11% to 38%. I also automated UPS and FedEx surcharge billing, cutting manual billing adjustments from ~200 per month to zero.
