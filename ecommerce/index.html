Matching Promotion Tool

In a tough economic climate, vendors wanted to run promotions to drive sales — but absorbing the full discount cost alone was a real barrier. The platform needed stronger price competitiveness to make FASHIONGO Week a genuine prime sales event.

I designed a co-funding model where the platform matches vendor discount contributions — reducing the burden on sellers while amplifying the promotional depth platform-wide. The result was the highest-revenue FASHIONGO Week in company history. What started as a one-time event tool proved so effective that vendors began using it independently year-round.

Label It — Industry-First Fulfillment Engine

The LA Fashion District jobber market is substantial — but every relabeling transaction happened entirely off-platform. Vendors, buyers, and relabeling partners were coordinating by phone call, managing defects and deliveries manually, and settling fees through paper invoices.

I identified this as an untapped revenue surface and architected a fulfillment domain to bring it on-platform. The system automatically handles order routing, defect notifications, shipping fee allocation, and multi-party settlement. We were the first wholesale platform to offer this.

03

Logistics · Payments · Settlement

Ship with FG — End-to-End Logistics Platform

Wholesale vendors were paying full market rate for every shipment — no leverage, no infrastructure, no automation. I built Ship with FG as a fully integrated logistics platform: not just a shipping rate tool, but a connected system spanning carrier integrations, automated settlement, refunds, and payment processing — all operating without manual intervention.

By consolidating vendor shipping volume across the platform, I negotiated rates up to 60–70% below industry average. When we expanded from UPS to FedEx, adoption jumped 35% in six months as vendors unlocked a high-AOV shipping segment previously inaccessible to them.

Embedded Payments · API Strategy · In Development

FG PAY — Headless Embedded Payment Platform

FASHIONGO's payment infrastructure was built to serve one platform — but the vendor network of 2,200+ sellers operates across multiple storefronts and marketplaces. The opportunity: make our payment rails portable.

I'm leading the ground-up development of FG PAY, a headless payment module with an API-first architecture that allows FASHIONGO's commerce payment capabilities to run natively on external vendor storefronts and partner marketplaces. This unbundles the payment layer from the core platform, creating a net-new transaction-fee revenue vertical.
